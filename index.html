Effortless, even for first-timers

The problem

Home BP monitors are fiddly — Bluetooth pairing, apps to open, settings to configure. Many patients, especially older ones, simply give up.

With Welfie

  • No Bluetooth pairing — nothing to connect or configure.
  • Data travels over audio — readings reach the phone through sound.
  • Launches automatically — the app opens when you switch the machine on.
